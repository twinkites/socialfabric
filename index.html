What's driving this

    Woven live from … RSS feeds across … languages. Each thread's color is the average sentiment of its recent headlines. Brightness tracks how much the world is talking about it.

    How the temperature is measured

    Every few hours, a background job reads RSS feeds spanning world news, culture, and markets in English, Spanish, and Chinese, plus search-based passes for the US state and county drill-downs. Each headline and summary is scored for sentiment: English with a lexicon-based VADER model, Spanish and Chinese with curated word-valence lexicons. Scores are averaged into one warmth score per cell. Warm colors mean recent coverage skews positive, cool colors mean it skews negative, and thread thickness tracks how much coverage a cell is getting. This isn't a poll or a survey. It's a rough, continuously updated read of the tone of public reporting, not of private sentiment. Full pipeline details, legal notice, and known limitations: METHODOLOGY.md.
